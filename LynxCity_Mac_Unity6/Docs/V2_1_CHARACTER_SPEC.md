# Lynx City V2.1 — Hero Character Specification

## Identity
Lynx is a 21-year-old Japanese university student in Tokyo, 1991. Height target: 177 cm. Build: lean and naturally athletic, not bodybuilder-sized. The face should be believable and individual rather than a stereotype or an anime design.

## Canonical visual direction
- Natural black medium-length hair appropriate to 1989–1992.
- Calm, observant default expression.
- Subtle asymmetry in the final sculpt/texture.
- Analog wristwatch; no modern wearable technology.
- University Casual is the canonical outfit.

## Five V2.1 outfits
1. University Casual (1991): ivory shirt, charcoal trousers, white court shoes, shoulder bag.
2. Denim Street (1989): washed denim jacket, pale tee, dark jeans, black trainers.
3. Varsity (1990): burgundy varsity jacket, cream sleeves, grey tee, navy jeans.
4. Smart Date (1992): dark blazer, light shirt, pleated trousers, polished shoes.
5. Night Leather (1989): black leather jacket, wine tee, faded jeans, dark boots.

## Animation acceptance bar
Before V2.2 street production begins, Lynx must look stable in idle, walk, run, start/stop and turns. Feet should not visibly slide at normal camera distance. Arms should not clip through the torso in the canonical outfit. Camera collision must not pass through nearby walls.

## Performance bar for the hero slice
The character lab targets a stable 60 fps on the development MacBook at its normal display resolution. The final hero art pipeline should use LODs and sensible texture budgets rather than relying on excessive 4K/8K assets.
